Cyber Threats

Recognize common threats before they become problems.

Common Cyber Threats

Phishing

Fake messages or websites designed to trick people into giving sensitive information.

How to stay safe:

Check the sender, website address and request carefully before clicking.

Malware

Malicious software that can damage devices, disrupt systems or compromise information.

How to stay safe:

Keep your software updated and download files only from trusted sources.

Ransomware

Malware that can make files or systems unavailable and demand payment.

How to stay safe:

Keep regular backups and install security updates.

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing information or taking unsafe actions.

How to stay safe:

Be careful with urgent requests, unfamiliar people and unexpected messages.

Password Attacks

Attempts to guess, steal or obtain passwords to access accounts.

How to stay safe:

Use long, unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.

Online Fraud

Deceptive online activities designed to obtain money or sensitive information.

How to stay safe:

Verify payment requests and never share passwords or OTPs.

What To Do When You Suspect a Cyber Threat

STOP

Do not immediately click, pay or share sensitive information.

VERIFY

Check the sender, website or request independently.

REPORT

Report suspicious activity through appropriate official channels.

Awareness is the first layer of defense.
