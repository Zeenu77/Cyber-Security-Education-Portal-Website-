What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting devices, networks, accounts and data from unauthorized access, misuse and digital attacks.

Core Concepts

Confidentiality

Only authorized people should be able to access information.

Integrity

Information should remain accurate and protected from unauthorized changes.

Availability

Systems and information should be accessible when they are needed.

Common Cyber Threats

Phishing

Fake emails, messages or websites that try to trick users into revealing information.

Malware

Malicious software designed to damage, disrupt or gain unauthorized access to systems.

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing information or performing unsafe actions.

Basic Safety Checklist

✓ Use unique and strong passwords.

✓ Enable multi-factor authentication when available.

✓ Keep your operating system and applications updated.

✓ Check links and senders before opening messages.

✓ Avoid sharing passwords and OTPs.

✓ Back up important files regularly.

Learn the basics. Practice the habits.
